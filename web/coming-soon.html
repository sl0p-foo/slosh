slosh

A terminal multiplexer. Panes, tabs, sessions you can detach from. Written in C on libghostty-vt.

Panes wear shaders: colour passes over live terminal cells. Not decoration: an unfocused pane dims, a dead shell greys out, scrollback tints, a bell shimmers until it is seen. Also scanlines and CRT phosphor, because they can. This pane is wearing two.
